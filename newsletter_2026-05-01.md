🌍 CONSTRUCTION TECH — TOP 5 THIS WEEK

📌 1. Triflex ProDetail / ProTerra — PMMA Cold Liquid Waterproofing | CR13
What it is: A two-component cold-applied PMMA liquid waterproofing system from German manufacturer Triflex. Reactive resin cures in roughly 30 minutes — walkable same shift, full traffic in two hours. Used heavily on flat roofs, balconies, planter boxes, and wet decks where downtime is expensive.
Why it matters to us: Fast cure = jobs we couldn't bid before because the building can't be shut for 7-day cure of bituminous systems. Live-traffic balconies on operating malls, hospital wet areas, hotel pool decks — premium pricing for time-sensitive work. Worth a discovery quote on our next mall or hospital R&R approach.
Read more:
· Triflex (manufacturer) — https://www.triflex.com
· Local distributor — search "Triflex Singapore distributor"

📌 2. Sika MonoTop 412 NFG — Structural Repair Mortar | CR09
What it is: One-component fibre-reinforced repair mortar for spalled concrete. Bonds without separate primer, reaches 30 MPa in 24 hours, certified for use in repairs of reinforced concrete structures (EN 1504-3 R4). Standard tool for HDB block spalling and beam soffit rectification.
Why it matters to us: Direct fit for NRP and MUP scopes — every Batch 12 NRP-style tender has spalling repair lines that get sub'd out today. Stocking this and bringing the technique in-house adds ~10–15% margin to any block A&A package. Aligns directly with the WCJW NRP tender we're tracking (closing 29 May).
Read more:
· Sika Singapore — https://www.sika.com.sg

📌 3. Buildots — AI Site Progress Tracking | CW01 / R&R
What it is: A 360° helmet camera (similar form factor to OpenSpace) but with deeper BIM-comparison AI. Walks the site once a week, then auto-flags every variance from the BIM plan — "wall not started", "door frame missing", "MEP penetration in wrong location". Used by Skanska, Vinci, Wates.
Why it matters to us: Defends progress claims with photographic + BIM evidence. On a 24-month NRP block, weekly Buildots scans cut ~50% off the doc effort for monthly progress claims and pre-empt VO disputes. Cheaper than employing an extra QS at scale.
Read more:
· Buildots — https://www.buildots.com

📌 4. Hilti ON!Track — Tool & Asset Tracking | All trades
What it is: BLE/NFC tag-based tool fleet management. Every drill, vacuum, level, generator gets a tag; mobile app shows where it is and who has it. Most contractors lose 3–7% of margin to tool shrinkage and hire-charges on multi-site work; this closes that hole.
Why it matters to us: With three workheads and active sites across HDB, JTC, and town council jobs, asset visibility is the cheapest margin recovery we can do. Pays back inside one year on a fleet our size, and the audit trail also satisfies BCA bizSAFE asset-control records.
Read more:
· Hilti ON!Track — https://www.hilti.com.sg

📌 5. Spot-r by Triax — Smart Hard Hat Wearable | All trades
What it is: Clip-on sensor for any hard hat that detects falls, slips, and impact events in real time and pushes alerts to the supervisor's phone. Also logs site headcount automatically (no manual roll call). Deployed on US commercial sites and now trialling in Hong Kong.
Why it matters to us: MOM has signalled extended Heightened Safety Period focus on falls from height — exactly our CR13 roof-work exposure. A wearable that flags a fall before the worker is found is both a real safety upgrade and a strong differentiator in pre-Q safety scoring. Worth a pilot on one CR13 roof job.
Read more:
· Triax Spot-r — https://www.triaxtec.com
