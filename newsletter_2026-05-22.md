🌍 CONSTRUCTION TECH — TOP 5 THIS WEEK

📌 1. Mapei Mapelastic Foundation — Two-Component Cementitious Waterproofing | CR13
What it is: Italian manufacturer Mapei's two-component cementitious-polymer membrane for below-grade and water-retention waterproofing. Trowel- or spray-applied at 2mm, crack-bridging to 0.75mm, certified EN 1504-2. Used on basements, lift pits, water tanks, and swimming pools.
Why it matters to us: Direct alternative to Sika Sikalastic on tank and basement scopes. Holding two compatible suppliers reduces single-vendor pricing exposure on NRP and JTC tank-relining jobs, and Mapei runs frequent contractor training in the region — fastest path to building in-house competency.
Read more:
· Mapei Singapore — https://www.mapei.com/sg

📌 2. Köster KSK SY 15 — Self-Adhesive Bituminous Membrane | CR13
What it is: German manufacturer Köster's cold self-adhesive 1.5mm bituminous sheet for below-grade waterproofing. Peel-and-stick application — no torch, no primer in most substrates. Targeted at basement walls, foundation slabs, and tunnel structures.
Why it matters to us: Removes the hot-works permit from below-grade scope entirely. On HDB block A&A and JTC factory basement jobs, that simplifies SCDF compliance and lets a smaller crew install faster. Worth quoting on the next basement tender as a bid differentiator.
Read more:
· Köster (manufacturer) — https://www.koster.eu

📌 3. PERI Skydeck — Modular Slab Formwork | CW01
What it is: German PERI's aluminium panel-and-prop modular slab formwork. Single-worker assembly, no scaffolding for spans up to 5m, roughly 40% faster cycle than traditional timber soffit + plywood. Used on commercial podiums and multi-storey carparks across the region.
Why it matters to us: For our CW01 small-building bids (community clubs, school A&A, polyclinic extensions), Skydeck collapses the slab pour cycle to about 5 days from 8. Faster cycle = either compressed programme as a bid differentiator, or higher margin at the same programme.
Read more:
· PERI Singapore — https://www.peri.com.sg

📌 4. DroneDeploy — Aerial Site Mapping for Roof and Façade | CW01 / CR13
What it is: Cloud-based drone-mapping platform that stitches aerial photos into high-resolution 3D site models and orthomosaics. Operates on consumer-grade DJI drones with CAAS permits. Used by Skanska, Lendlease, and several local main contractors.
Why it matters to us: For CR13 roof surveys and CW01 progress reporting, a single 20-minute flight replaces a half-day of scaffolded inspection — and the model becomes the basis for an as-built record. The roof leak detection workflow alone justifies the cost within one job.

· DroneDeploy — https://www.dronedeploy.com

What it is: Cloud platform for daily reports, RFIs, drawings, punch lists, and progress photos on mobile. Replaces the WhatsApp + email chain that dominates most local sites with a structured record. Procore opened a Singapore office in 2024 and has been signing mid-tier contractors actively.
Why it matters to us: With three workheads and concurrent sites, the audit trail alone defends us on variation claims and progress-payment disputes. The free pilot tier covers two active projects — low-cost way to test before standardising. Pairs naturally with the DroneDeploy workflow above.

🦺 SAFETY REMINDERS — TOOLBOX TALKS FOR NEXT WEEK
Heading into the year's peak inter-monsoon rain period — wet roofs, slick scaffolds, lightning risk. Brief every crew on weather-stop protocols.

• CR13 waterproofing — Edge protection, harness anchor verified, no solo work. Hot-applied membranes: hot-works permit, fire watch, extinguisher within 10m. Solvent and PMMA primers: ventilation plus organic-vapour respiratory PPE.
• CR09 repair & redecoration — Electrical isolation before tool change. Silica and gypsum dust: wet cutting or LEV, P3 mask. Two-person lift on anything over 25 kg.
• CW01 general building — Scaffold green-tag check every shift start. Lifting operations only with permit and signaller. Site induction for every new worker, no exceptions.
• All trades — Risk Assessment documents must be dated this quarter. Lightning protocol: stop external work when thunder is heard within 30 seconds of a flash. Resume only 30 minutes after the last strike.
