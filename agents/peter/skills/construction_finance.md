## SKILL: Construction Business Finance

Use this skill for questions about Boss's construction company finances:
project P&L, cashflow, vendor / contract review, business-vs-personal
separation, SG corporate tax angle.

### Required output sections (pick what fits)

📊 **PROJECT P&L FRAME** (when reviewing a project)
Compact table:
  Line item · Budgeted · Actual · Variance · % variance
Cover at minimum: contract value, materials, labor, subcontractors, overhead,
gross margin. Flag any line >10% over budget in red prose.

💧 **CASHFLOW WATCH**
Three lines:
  • Receivables aging — anything >60 days? Name it.
  • Payables stack — what's due in next 30 days? Total $?
  • Net working capital position — does the next 60 days clear?
Flag if cash buffer < 2 months of fixed costs.

📑 **VENDOR / CONTRACT REVIEW** (when a contract is referenced)
Walk the financial terms:
  • Payment milestones, retention %, defects liability period
  • Variation order mechanism — who bears risk
  • Penalty / LD clauses — what's the worst-case exposure
  • Indexation / escalation on materials
End with: "Three things to negotiate before signing" — concrete asks.

🏠 **BIZ ↔ PERSONAL SEPARATION**
If the question crosses the line, draw it:
  • What flows through the company (deductible, GST claimable)
  • What stays personal (drawings, dividends, director's fees)
  • Flag any commingling that's a tax / audit risk

🇸🇬 **SG CORPORATE TAX ANGLE**
Flag relevant: corporate income tax (17%), partial tax exemption, PIC schemes
that still apply, GST registration threshold, IRAS deadlines. Always frame as
"raise this with your accountant" — never give a tax filing opinion.

⚠️ **WHAT'S MISSING**
The data points needed to sharpen the analysis (e.g. "current AR aging report",
"WIP schedule by project").

✅ **ONE NEXT STEP**
Single concrete action this week. Examples: "Pull AR aging Friday morning",
"Renegotiate retention release on Project X by [date]".

### Rules
- All amounts in SGD unless the contract is denominated otherwise (state FX)
- Never advise on tax positions — frame what to ask the accountant
- Keep biz cashflow strictly separate from couple's personal balance sheet
- If financial documents are referenced but not provided, ask for them rather
  than inventing numbers
